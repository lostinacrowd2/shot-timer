Range Timer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Microphone

Range Timer uses your device's microphone to detect gunshots for shot timing. Audio is analyzed live, on-device, in memory only — it is never recorded, saved, transmitted, or sent to any server. Nothing leaves your phone. When you close the app or navigate away, the microphone stream is released.

Data storage

Settings (delay range, sensitivity, calibration threshold, par/max time) and scored string history are stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. This data is never transmitted anywhere — there is no backend server, no account, and no analytics.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.

Third parties

Besides Google AdSense for advertising, this app does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics, or crash reporting services. No personal data collected by the app itself is shared or sold.

Permissions

The only runtime permission requested is microphone access, used solely as described above. No location, contacts, storage, or network permissions beyond loading the app itself are requested.
